# Competitive read: the ten "hireEZ alternatives" — and what they change for TalentOS

Date: 2026-09-05. Desk research only (vendor pages, review aggregators, pricing
trackers, press releases). No product was trialled. Companion to
`COMPETITIVE-2026-09-05.md` (Metaview / hireEZ / Heartbeat.ai) and the action
plan in `ACTION-PLAN-2026-09-05.md`; this document does not reopen decisions
made there. It adds ten more products to the scorecard and states where the
existing thesis still holds, where it needs sharpening, and what is new.

Source list: https://www.goperfect.com/blog/10-best-hireez-alternatives-for-ai-recruiting-in-2026

**Read the list for what it is.** GoPerfect wrote it, ranks itself first, lists
no cons for itself, and frames the category around its own pitch ("inbound
screening + outbound in one tool"). Three of the ten are not sourcing tools at
all (Humanly, HireVue, Eightfold), one no longer exists as an independent
product (Entelo → Rival Recruit), and the list omits the products that actually
compete with GoPerfect on its own terms (Metaview, Pin, Noon, LinkedIn
Recruiter, hireEZ's own agent). The article's evaluation axes — sourcing,
inbound screening, outbound, explainable scoring, ATS count, pricing
transparency — are still a useful lens because they are what the market is
advertising in 2026.

## 1. The ten, one at a time

Prices are third-party observations unless a vendor publishes them; every one
of the ten except Juicebox, Fetcher and SeekOut Core is demo-gated.

### 1. GoPerfect — "autonomous end-to-end recruiting"

- **What it is.** Sources from an aggregated 800M+ profile index, screens
  inbound applicants through the ATS, sends outreach by email, LinkedIn and
  SMS. Every candidate gets an "explainable 1–5 Match Score" with AI-written
  reasons. 60+ ATS integrations. Founded 2023.
- **Pricing.** Article says "$250–300 per position per month". Third parties
  say ~$250 per user-month on annual contract, **two-seat minimum, ~$6,000/yr
  floor**, plus ~150 credits/month for contact unlocks and outreach with
  overage exposure. Nothing on the pricing page.
- **Human in the loop.** Humans "take over when candidates reply" — that is,
  after the agent has already sourced, scored and sent.
- **Weaknesses on record.** Demo-gated pricing; unpredictable credit overages;
  young company with thin review footprint and limited integration depth;
  LinkedIn + email + SMS only; US-centric index subject to decay.

### 2. SeekOut — "talent intelligence + diversity sourcing"

- **What it is.** 1B+ profiles across seven verticals (public profiles, GitHub,
  academic, healthcare, nursing, ATS, internal). Boolean + AI Assist, deep
  technical parsing, patents and publications. "Sam" async AI interviewer.
  **SeekOut MCP** (2026): search, fourteen guided workflows, save candidates,
  all from inside Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Copilot, returning links that
  open in the SeekOut UI. SeekOut Spot: managed agentic recruiting service.
- **Pricing.** Recruit Core $179/mo ($149 annual), 500 verified contacts and
  1,000 exports per month, no annual lock. Everything above is custom,
  $3K–$15K+/yr on the low end and $10K–$90K+/yr in enterprise.
- **Weaknesses on record.** 4.5/5 on 759 G2 reviews, but: expensive, credits
  don't roll over, learning curve, "not ideal for agencies". **Diversity
  classifiers infer gender and ethnicity from names, schools and group
  membership, not self-report; G2 reviewers flag misclassification,
  especially for non-Western names.** Phone enrichment weak.

### 3. Eightfold AI — "deep learning talent intelligence"

- **What it is.** Enterprise talent graph over "1.6B+ career profiles" and
  "1.6M+ skills": skills inference, internal mobility, workforce planning,
  1–5 candidate scoring, bi-directional ATS/HRIS/VMS/LMS sync, FedRAMP
  Moderate.
- **Pricing.** Quote-only. ~$7–10 per employee per month; $70K–$100K/yr for a
  1,000-person company; realistically for 2,000–10,000+ employees.
- **Weaknesses on record.** Weeks-to-months implementation, dense UI, support
  turnover, opaque weighting inside the deep-learning match, and — the one
  that matters here — **"not built for active sourcing"**: no passive-candidate
  discovery, no outreach. It is an HR platform, not a recruiter's tool.

### 4. Fetcher — "automated outbound sourcing + outreach"

- **What it is.** AI-generated candidate batches reviewed by human sourcing
  specialists, then automated email sequences with A/B testing, multi-language,
  calendar sync. Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, JazzHR, Merge API.
- **Pricing.** Published: Growth $499/mo ($379 annual), Amplify $849/mo ($649
  annual), Enterprise custom; another tracker shows tiers from $115/mo. Vendr
  median contract $11,000/yr (range $8,402–$26,000). Growth is capped at
  **500 sourced candidates per year** and 2,500 inbound reviews. SOC 2 and SSO
  are Enterprise-only. No free trial.
- **Weaknesses on record.** "Inconsistent candidate match quality", misses on
  seniority, inflexible templates, slowdowns in heavy hiring periods, no
  resume parsing or match scores.

### 5. Gem — "recruiting CRM + embedded sourcing"

- **What it is.** CRM + ATS + sourcing over 800M+ profiles + sequences +
  scheduling + analytics. Three 2026 agents: **AI Sourcing Agent**
  (Ideal Profiles, funding insights), **AI Application Review Agent**
  ("transparent match reasoning", EEO-guideline bias warnings, **annual
  third-party bias audits with BABL**), **AI Rediscovery** (past applicants,
  silver medalists, old scorecards and notes resurfaced for new roles).
  Fraud Detection agent announced.
- **Pricing.** FTE-headcount-based, custom. Published floor $99/user-month
  (staffing Essentials) and $270/mo (Startups in-house); startups under 30
  employees free for six months then 50% off. Vendr median **$24,900/yr**,
  range $7,000–$70,955; SMB average $19,832, enterprise average $94,560.
- **Weaknesses on record.** 4.8/5 on G2, the category's best, but: cost
  scales with company size not recruiting need; sourcing-only buyers "pay for
  the whole platform's shape"; configuration overhead; some integrations and
  analytics gated to enterprise tiers.

### 6. Findem — "attribute-based people search"

- **What it is.** Deterministic search over "hundreds of aggregated data
  attributes" (skills demonstrated over time, career progression, project
  contributions) rather than keywords; 3D data model. **Acquired Glider AI
  (announced 2026-03-19)**: skills validation, autonomous AI interviews and
  identity verification. Introduced **outcome-aligned pricing tied to hires**.
  Greenhouse, Lever, Bullhorn, Jobvite, SmartRecruiters, BambooHR.
- **Pricing.** ~$8,000/yr floor, $25K–$100K/yr mid-market; annual only
  (3-month option for the sourcing module); 4–6 week implementation; no trial.
- **Weaknesses on record.** Enterprise pricing and minimums, "not suited for
  SMBs or ad hoc hiring", email-only contact data, no phone enrichment,
  unsuitable for hourly roles.

### 7. Humanly — "conversational AI candidate screening"

- **What it is.** Chatbot-led screening and scheduling over SMS, chat, email,
  voice and video for teams hiring 1,000+ hourly/frontline workers a year;
  AI Recruiter, AI Interviewer, Talent CRM and ATS.
- **Pricing.** Quote-only; $15K–$50K+/yr mid-market.
- **Weaknesses on record.** Text screening carries no communication signal
  for customer-facing roles; multi-week implementation; pushes SMBs up-market;
  "buying a CRM to answer a contact form". **Not a sourcing tool.**

### 8. Entelo → Rival Recruit — "predictive sourcing"

- **What it is.** Acquired by SilkRoad Technology (now Rival HR) in 2022; one
  module of a four-product HR suite. 750M+ profiles, "More Likely To Move"
  attrition propensity, ML-inferred diversity sourcing (gender, ethnicity,
  veteran status "without relying on self-reported data"), Greenhouse /
  Workable / Lever / iCIMS.
- **Pricing.** ~$125–149 per user-month starter (250 profiles/month),
  enterprise negotiated, no trial, no public page.
- **Weaknesses on record.** 3.7/5 on Capterra from 18 reviews; "expensive
  with results that were not there"; thin non-tech coverage; weak EMEA email
  under GDPR; product direction uncertain since acquisition.

### 9. HireVue — "AI-powered video interviewing"

- **What it is.** On-demand and live video interviews, game-based and coding
  assessments, AI-assisted evaluation of transcribed answers (facial analysis
  discontinued 2021), structured interview guides. Needs a separate ATS.
- **Pricing.** Quote-only, annual auto-renewing, multi-year typical.
  $25K–$50K/yr mid-market, $50K–$150K enterprise, $150K–$300K+ large;
  $5–$30 per interview.
- **Weaknesses on record.** Candidate experience "alienating", video-request
  response rates 50–70%, AI scoring opacity is a legal and reputational risk,
  implementation in weeks. **Not a sourcing tool.**

### 10. Juicebox (PeopleGPT) — "natural language people search"

- **What it is.** Plain-English search over a global profile index, ranked
  results "with the matching evidence highlighted". Chrome extension for
  LinkedIn, Gmail and Outlook. **Juicebox Agents (launched 2026-05-20)**: take
  a JD, "develop their search strategy, discuss trade-offs", ask clarifying
  questions ("PLG or demo-led growth?"), run for weeks, self-edit their
  search from feedback, auto-shortlist and write multi-step outreach.
- **Pricing.** Published: Starter $99/seat-month annual ($119 monthly) with
  500 contact + 500 export credits; Growth $179 ($199 monthly) with 1,500 each,
  five-seat ceiling; Business custom, unlimited, annual only. **Agents $199
  per agent per month.** Free tier and trial exist.
- **Weaknesses on record.** Results "skew toward candidates with big public
  footprints — the same people every other tool surfaces"; vague brief →
  vague list; English-only; cost creep to $300–$400/mo for heavy users;
  thin workflow depth; almost no G2/Capterra footprint; **multiple
  practitioners report LinkedIn account suspensions after using the Chrome
  extension for sourcing.**

## 4. What this changes in the program

Nothing here reopens the wave order (A closed, B D E P F G open). It sharpens
four waves and adds three backlog items.

**Wave B (calibration).** Ship it with the visible reason line — "widened
_staff engineer_ → +_principal_: 3 accepted anchors, 0 dismissed". That single
line is what separates it from Juicebox's chat-recalibrate and Metaview's
opaque loop. Without it, Wave B is just another black box.

**Wave E (registry identity).** Wording: "registry-matched · CMS NPPES ·
2026-09-05", never "verified". Findem and Glider now own the plain word in
buyers' heads and it means something else.

**Wave G (transparency).** Three additions from this research:

1. Publish the cost model beside the ten list prices above (all public,
   all cited). Nobody else on the list can print that table.
2. Extend "what we will not do" with three lines each of which is a
   competitor's complaint: _no inferred diversity attributes_ (SeekOut,
   Entelo); _no browser-extension scraping_ (Juicebox suspensions); _no
   autonomous outreach_ (GoPerfect, Juicebox Agents).
3. Replace every use of "explainable" in product copy with "verifiable" and
   say what it means: the quote is in the document, click it.

**Browser companion (uncommitted Codex work, `BROWSER_COMPANION.md`).** Add a
guardrail before it lands: capture only on an explicit recruiter save, never
on page load; per-site opt-in; a plain warning that LinkedIn suspends accounts
for extension-driven sourcing. Juicebox is the cautionary case.

**Backlog additions** (appended to `BACKLOG.md`, not built — anti-drift):

1. **Rediscovery first.** Before any external discovery run, rank the
   recruiter's own saved candidates against the new search's canonical
   requirements using the existing anchor machinery. Gem sells this as an
   agent; locally it is a query.
2. **TalentOS as a local MCP server.** Expose the search context, requirement
   set and "validate and import suggestions" as tools a Claude session can
   call. Inverts SeekOut MCP (their data into the assistant; here the
   assistant into the recruiter's local data), removes the outbox
   copy-paste, keeps D-008 intact because the session, not the app, holds
   the model. Already half-specified by the "session provider in the UI"
   item.
3. **Determinism metric.** Re-run a stored string N minutes later and record
   overlap; the ledger already has the shape. Publish the number next to the
   14% figure.

## 5. What not to chase (reaffirmed, with the list's names attached)

- A profile index — GoPerfect, SeekOut, Gem, Findem, Entelo, Fetcher,
  Juicebox all sell the same one and the reviewers say so.
- Contact enrichment credits — the universal complaint.
- Autonomous agents that send — GoPerfect, Juicebox Agents, Fetcher.
- Video or chatbot screening — HireVue, Humanly. Different problem, different
  buyer, heavy regulatory exposure.
- Enterprise talent graph / internal mobility — Eightfold. Wrong buyer.
- Inferred diversity — SeekOut, Entelo. Forbidden by rule 4 and, in the EU
  from 2027-12-02, a high-risk deployer obligation.
